Process

No mystery, no disappearing. Here's exactly what happens after you message me.

You send the details

What you want built or fixed, any examples you like, and your deadline. Screenshots and rough sketches are totally fine.

02

I reply with a plan & price

A short, clear message: what I'll do, how long it'll take, and what it costs. No hourly billing confusion.

03

You get a live preview link early so you can see progress and request changes while it's cheap to change things.

Launch & handover

Deployed, tested on mobile, and you get the full code. If something breaks within two weeks of delivery, I fix it free.

About

A bit about me

I'm a self-taught developer who cares more about things working than things looking impressive in a slide deck.

I write plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — code you can actually read and maintain later. When a project needs a backend, I use Firebase so there's no server to babysit. I keep scopes small because small scopes get finished.

If you have a page that's almost right, a bug you can't pin down, or an idea you want live by next week — that's exactly the kind of work I do best.
